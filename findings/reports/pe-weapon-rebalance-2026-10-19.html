Days 2–4

Each logged battle with Aya's real level and armor at that point, and the Day 4 class guns swapped in. The G3A3 is already the Day 4 rifle. Rows marked "+ Attack All" are the same gun with a catalog-installed Attack All.

  • Rifle: 32.7 → 25.2 s per ordinary fight (pack 22–28 s); boss fights 89 → 59 s.
  • Attack All guns: about 1.2–1.5× faster than without it after R-A, against about 1.7–1.8× now.
  • Win rates: unchanged or better everywhere.

Day 5

Each class's representative Day 5 gun, Aya from the same campaign build for every class.

  • The pack closes up: every class lands at 24–32 s, against 25–48 s now.
  • Rifle bosses: the one Day 5 boss loss disappears at typical and high investment.
  • Grenade: vanilla's elemental resist (damage ÷5) makes the frost-firing M203 4 crawl; the mod's 80% resist already fixes most of that, and R-C finishes the job.

Disc 2

Wayne's endgame guns with 2 Commands and Quickdraw, plus the LAW80 rocket launcher and a MAG with a catalog Attack All.

  • Attack All guns: about 1.5× faster than single-target guns after R-A, against 2.1–2.6× now.
  • Bosses: unchanged; R-A never applies to boss fights.
  • LAW80: fast in crowds but ammo-gated: a main-gun LAW80 needs about 190 rockets over Disc 2; the game gives 9, and 20 Junk each buys about a third more.
  • Absolute boss numbers are directional: in four logged boss fights the player held fire or used the club.

Why R-A became a cap

Every Attack All rule tried, on the same fights at typical investment. The goal is Attack All about 1.5× faster than single-target fire at every stage, never slower than the same gun without it.

  • Power cuts subtract defense after the cut, so weak Day 2–5 guns fall to the 1-damage floor. The old R-A made Day 5 Attack All guns 1.5–3.5× slower than without it.
  • After-defense and crowd-scaled cuts fix Day 5 but don't bite in Disc 2, where the P90 still kills every enemy in one volley.
  • The 75% HP cap targets overkill directly: a crowd needs two Attack All turns. 60% goes too far (some Attack All guns get slower than without it).

Mods

Value of each mod on its own: change in fight time versus no mod, averaged over the classes (negative is faster). R-E rows show the proposed rate-of-fire rule.

  • Why rate of fire fails today: each extra bullet replays the gun's follow animation: 30 frames on a shotgun, 25 on a grenade launcher, 15 on a rifle, 3 on a machine gun. Extra power alone can't fix it; even 225% volleys left RoF 7 and 10 slower.
  • R-E applies to installed mods only, so built-in RoF guns like the P90 and MP5PDW keep their balance.
  • The mod's element, status and Critical changes work: vanilla Heat and Frost slow Day 5 fights by 36% and 107%.
  • Healthy roles: Commands are boss mods, Quickdraw is the crowd mod.

Parasite Energy

The mod's combat rules with its PE changes swapped out, to isolate them.

The heal percentages rarely matter: at Day 2–4 max HP they land close to vanilla's flat heals, and the player heals only below half HP. Haste, a source rule worth knowing, cuts Aya's defense to 30% while it lasts.

How the comparison works

Simulator
Combat rebuilt from the decompiled game: Aya's offense, defense, statuses, Parasite Energy and items, every enemy's own attacks, body-contact damage, scripted boss HP.
Calibration
Every logged battle replayed: ordinary damage 1.05× of the log (correlation 0.77), bosses 0.66× (0.82). Energy Shot and Liberate match live casts exactly.
Paired fights
Vanilla, mod and package fight the same battles with the same seeds and builds.
Investment
Attack bonus players actually reach, from the economy simulator: Day 2–4 +9 / +15 / +20, Day 5 +12 / +18 / +25, Disc 2 +35 / +50 / +68.
Rule sets
Vanilla: original rules and PE. Current mod: element resist 80%, status 60%, critical conversion, Quickdraw +15, RoF 7 / 10 boost, half-bar items and powers, percentage heals. Package: the current mod plus R-A, R-B, R-C and R-E.
Wins
Every enemy dead before Aya, within 90 s (400 s for bosses). Average times include losses.

Building it

  • R-B, R-C: weapon-table edits (G3A3 base attack +45, M203 4 +15).
  • R-A: in the existing pe_on_damage hook (0x80028574): per action, remember each target's HP; once a target has lost 75% of its max HP this turn (2+ enemies alive, no boss bit 0x40000), stop further Attack All damage to it; check the last hit on the next battle update. Menu row "Attack All crowd cap %", default 75.
  • R-E: for installed RoF 5 / 7 / 10 mods, raise per-bullet power through atk_pm, speed up Aya's animation during the volley (as the battle-start speed feature does), and zero the gun type's per-shot hold in g_BattleActionAnimRows (0x800106A4) for the volley. Menu row "RoF mods: fast volleys".
  • Every change behind a Mod Effects row, with vanilla one click away. Test order: R-B / R-C, then R-A, then R-E, each against the matching save states.
