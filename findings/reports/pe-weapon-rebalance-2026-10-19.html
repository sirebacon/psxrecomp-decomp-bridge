Parasite Eve Weapon Rebalance

Vanilla rules, the current mod and the proposed adjustments, compared fight for fight on the calibrated simulator. Every weapon class is run through every battle from the logged playthrough, at Day 5 and in the Disc 2 endgame.

Calibrated model: ordinary-battle damage at 1.05× of the play log 5 classes × 3 seeds × every logged battle 3 player-assumption sets

The short version

The mod already fixes the worst vanilla problems: the Day 5 grenade launcher stops failing, and most Disc 2 fights get shorter and safer. That leaves three imbalances in the current mod, and each has a small, targeted fix.

R-A · CROWD CUT

Attack All at 40% with 2+ enemies, never vs bosses

The P90 clears Disc 2 crowds 2.4–3× faster than any other class and takes almost no damage. At 40% it stays the crowd specialist (about 1.4× faster) without making the other guns pointless.

REVISED: 40% again, not 30%

P90 ordinary: 4.9 s → 8.6 s · others 11.9–14.9 s
R-B · RIFLE

G3A3 attack +45

The Day 5 rifle is the slowest class by a wide margin. With +45 it lands in the middle of the Day 5 pack, and takes half the damage it did.

CONFIRMED

R-C · GRENADE

Day 5: class by class

Each class uses its representative Day 5 gun with Day 5 attack investment and only its built-in effects. Aya's level, armor and stats are the same campaign build for every class, so only the gun differs. Times are the average fight length; damage is HP lost per fight.

What changes between the three rule sets

  • Vanilla → mod, grenade. The M203 4 fires frost rounds. Vanilla cuts elemental damage to a fifth against resistant enemies, and many Day 5 enemies resist frost, so fights run into the 90-second cap: only 40% of ordinary fights finish. The mod's resist (80% damage instead of 20%) makes the class viable.
  • Vanilla → mod, machine gun. The mod's RoF 7/10 per-bullet boost and critical conversion take the MP5PDW from 32.2 s to 28.5 s.
  • Handgun and shotgun are unchanged by the mod at Day 5: no element, no RoF 7/10, no Quickdraw at this stage.
  • Mod → proposal. Only the G3A3 and the M203 4 change. Afterwards every Day 5 class clears ordinary battles in 27–33 s, against 27–49 s now.
  • Bosses. The rifle stays the slowest boss gun (65 s vs 42–63 s for the others) and loses 1 boss fight in 24 under all three rule sets. That loss is one specific Day 5 boss fight, not a balance problem the proposal should chase.

Disc 2: class by class

Endgame guns from Wayne with equal investment: attack, range and capacity boosts of 30 / 10 / 10, plus 2 Commands and Quickdraw (the universal core of a finished build). 92 ordinary battles and 13 boss battles from the log, three seeds each.

Reading the Disc 2 numbers

  • Vanilla → mod is a modest across-the-board improvement: ordinary fights about 5% shorter, damage taken down 10–30%, more boss fights won (machine 0.97 → 1.00, rifle 0.92 → 1.00, handgun 0.92 → 0.97).
  • The P90 problem predates the mod. Vanilla Attack All already clears crowds in 5.8 s against 12.5–15.7 s for the others; the mod's RoF 10 boost sharpens it to 4.9 s.
  • The MAG's boss time looks worse in the mod (40 s → 47 s), but vanilla loses 3 of 39 boss fights with it, and losses end early. The mod wins all of them; the longer average is the cost of surviving, not a slowdown.
  • Boss columns don't move under the proposal. Attack All's cut is off whenever a boss is present, and the Disc 2 guns aren't touched by R-B or R-C.

R-A in detail: how hard to cut Attack All

The cut applies per bullet when Attack All fires, at least two enemies are alive and no boss is present. Two strengths were run on the same fights.

Why 40% and not 30%. An earlier model (before contact damage, Parasite Energy and the full enemy roster) suggested 30%. On the calibrated model, 30% overshoots. The P90 becomes no faster than the handgun (12.6 s vs 11.9 s) while taking the most damage of any class (11.3 vs 6.1–9.2), so there would be no reason to carry it. At 40% it keeps a clear crowd edge (8.6 s, about 1.4× the handgun) and normal exposure (4.1 damage). The same ordering holds under the slow-menu and keep-distance assumptions.

The campaign-earned builds agree. The controlled P90 build goes from 3.3 s to 9.4 s at 30%, level with the other classes (9.4–10.3 s), but takes 8.2 damage against 2.0–4.3.

Boss fights

Win rate and average length of boss fights per class. Day 5 bosses are well modelled (0.91× of logged damage). Disc 2 bosses are directional: in the log the player held fire or switched to the club for many turns in four of those fights, so simulated fights there are shorter and absolute damage is lower than the real thing.

How robust is this

The recommendations should not depend on one guess about how people play. Each run was repeated under three player-assumption sets (use the switch above to see every table and chart under each):

Calibrated player
Fitted to the logged playthrough: a 1-second paused menu per turn; enemies can't start attacks while Aya's action is committed (game flag 0x100).
Slow menus
+75 battle updates (2.5 s) of real time per turn before acting.
Keeps distance
Enemy distances × 1.4: more grazes and misses, fewer melee hits.

Under all three, R-B and R-C put the rifle and grenade launcher inside the Day 5 pack, and 40% keeps the P90 fastest without dominating. Under slow menus the 30% variant gets worse (P90 15.0 s and 21.9 damage, the most of any class), which strengthens the case for 40%.

How the comparison works

Simulator
Source-level combat rebuilt from the decompiled game. Aya's offense, defense, statuses, Parasite Energy and items are all from game code. Every enemy in the log has its attacks from its own room scripts, plus body-contact damage.
Calibration
Replaying every logged battle: ordinary damage 1.05× of the log (correlation 0.77), bosses 0.66× (correlation 0.82). Aya's damage per turn matches the log in every group. Energy Shot and Liberate match live casts exactly.
Paired fights
Vanilla, mod and proposal fight the same battle with the same seed, build and assumption set, so differences are not sampling noise. Re-running the same rule set in a separate process reproduces ordinary results exactly and boss averages within about 1%.
Rule sets
Vanilla: original rules and Parasite Energy heals. Current mod: elemental resist 80% (from 20%), neutral elements ×1.15 and best-of-two, status chance 60%, poison +25%, critical conversion, Quickdraw +15, RoF 7 / 10 bullets 20 / 15% (from 18 / 13), half-bar items, powers and weapon switch, percentage heals. Proposal: the current mod plus R-A, R-B and R-C.
Wins
A fight counts as won if every enemy dies before Aya does and within 90 s (400 s for bosses). Average times include lost fights, which usually end early.
Parasite Energy
The generic policy: heal below half HP with the best known heal, medicine from the battle's logged bag, Haste on the first boss turn. Spells and items are the ones the logged player had at that point.

Implementing the three changes

R-A · Attack All crowd cut

It fits the existing per-hit hook: pe_mod_effects.c pe_on_damage at the entry of Battle_StepEnemyMovement (0x80028574), which already scales the attack term through atk_pm.

  • Attack All: (turn & 0xC0) == 0xC0 (Battle_FillActionQueue mode 0xC0).
  • Two or more active enemy slots with HP above 0 (slots at PE_ENEMY_SLOT_BASE, 7 × 0xDC).
  • No active slot with status2 bit 0x40000, the "can't escape" boss bit the loot mods already read.
  • Then atk_pm = atk_pm * 400 / 1000, multiplying with the existing RoF 7 / 10 boost.
  • Add a Mod Effects row "Attack All power % (crowds)", default 40, vanilla 100.

R-B and R-C · weapon table edits

  • G3A3 base attack +45.
  • M203 4 base attack +20.

Both are single weapon-table edits and touch no other gun (the MAG and HK40 are unchanged).
